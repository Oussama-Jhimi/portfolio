About Me

Embedded Systems Engineer

Hello! I'm Oussama, an Embedded Systems Engineer with expertise in microcontroller programming, circuit design, and real-time systems. I specialize in developing efficient, reliable embedded solutions for various applications.

My skills span across multiple platforms including STM32, ESP32, Arduino, and Raspberry Pi, with proficiency in C, C++, Python, Linux and Assembly languages.

When I'm not working on embedded projects, I enjoy exploring new hardware technologies, contributing to open-source embedded projects, and learning about the latest developments in IoT and robotics.
