New Product with MushloveLab

PCB design and development with Altium Designer, integrating sensors and IoT modules

Oussama Jhimi
Sept. 2025 - Oct. 2025
PCB Design & IoT

Innovative device enabling real-time measurement of CO₂, temperature, and humidity, featuring instant display and remote monitoring & control via an IoT app.

Advanced PCB Design & Hardware Optimization

  • 2 layer PCB design: Signal, power, and ground planes for improved EMI/EMC performance.
  • Optimized component placement: Routing for sensor accuracy and low-noise analog measurements.
  • Power management design: (LDO / DC-DC) ensuring stable operation and low power consumption.
  • Thermal-aware layout: To enhance reliability and extend component lifetime.
  • Design for Manufacturing (DFM) & DFA: To ensure production-ready hardware.
  • Protection circuits: (ESD, reverse polarity, overcurrent) for industrial-grade robustness.

OTA (Over-The-Air) updates

Updates via HTTP server

  • Rapid deployment: Of new features and bug fixes without physical intervention.
  • Secure and continuous maintenance: Minimizing risks of manual updates.
  • Enhanced product durability: And scalability.
  • Cost reduction: Maintenance cost reduction of up to 30–40% compared to traditional update methods.

Key Applications

Mushroom Cultivation

Optimize yield by maintaining ideal CO₂ and humidity levels.

Agriculture

Monitor temperature and adjust conditions for optimal growth.

Energy Efficiency

Reduce costs by automatically adjusting ventilation and heating.

Precision

Accurate data for smarter and more sustainable crop management.
